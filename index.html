Notation and hypotheses

N₊ and N₋ denote future and past null incompleteness. T₊ and T₋ denote the timelike counterparts. N = N₊ ∨ N₋ and T = T₊ ∨ T₋. A negation denotes completeness in the stated sector and orientation.

V is the chronology-violating set, C means V is empty, and B means its boundary has an almost closed incomplete null generator. B₋ specifies past incompleteness. A denotes the existence of a nonempty compact achronal edgeless hypersurface. Y is the Yamabe class of future conformal infinity. MOTS means marginally outer trapped surface.

The conditions shown summarize the source hypotheses. D005–D046 and D057 fix the same Cauchy hypersurface Σ. Apply the remaining hypotheses of the first source if Σ is noncompact and of the second if Σ is compact, in a common admissible dimension and regularity class. For D051–D054 retain T084 and all hypotheses of the second source except chronology. A₊ denotes a future-incomplete achronal null ray.

Additional hypothesis for D014–D022 and D026–D028. Let FF mean that Σ\Sigma is diffeomorphic to a spherical space form. For T013 and T014, genericity requires a point of nonzero transverse tidal curvature on each future or past inextendible null half-geodesic issuing normally from the surface, on the cover for T014 [4]. A point somewhere on its bilateral extension does not suffice.

Chronology rule. By T084 (Kriele, Theorem 4 and Corollary 7), assume a connected four-dimensional spacetime, compact closure and nonempty complement of V, with null convergence and the generic condition on a nonempty compact connected component W of the chronology boundary in the nonchronological branch. In the chronological branch impose every hypothesis of the second cited theorem except chronology itself. Then ¬C⇒B\neg C\Rightarrow B and C⇒N∨TC\Rightarrow N\lor T imply ¬N⇒C∧T,¬T⇒(C∧N)∨(¬C∧B).\neg N\Rightarrow C\land T,\qquad \neg T\Rightarrow (C\land N)\lor(\neg C\land B). For the remaining rows retain the geometric source assumptions except for the alternatives explicitly resolved in the row. In D003 impose the repaired T035 specific nonempty trapped-surface horismos identity. In D004 spatial openness is that of T036. D048 and D054 use both nonpositive future null expansions and the normal generic condition of T063. D050 uses strong causality, the specific trapped-surface horismos identity, and either the MH property or the nonempty horizon of an edgeless achronal boundary, omitting the no-coexistence premise of T038/T039. D055 assumes chronology, the causal convergence and generic conditions, past causal simplicity and the finite past-volume condition of T008. D056 retains all assumptions of T027 and T028 apart from the sign of the boundary Yamabe constant.

In the future-complete rigidity branch of T033 the outward generators lie in a totally geodesic null hypersurface. Its null Weingarten map vanishes, so the optical Riccati equation forces the transverse tidal operator to vanish along those generators. The normal genericity required by T013 on each future half-generator is therefore incompatible with this branch [R4,R53]. A nonvacuous rigidity-plus-past-incompleteness statement would need an independent past-incompleteness criterion compatible with outward rigidity. The formal implication alone provides no such criterion.
