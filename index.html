The network distinguishes documented source-supported reductions, calculated transitive paths, and unresolved comparisons. It is not a machine-verified formal proof database. Shared tags alone cannot certify logical implications.
Research tool: theorems may be connected by proved reductions only where the cited publication supports the mapping of hypotheses and conclusions.
